## One enhancement I'd like to add

Given your background and goals, I'd also include a **Stage 0 — Database Mindset Reset** before Stage 1. Since you've been working heavily with SQL for the past seven months, this short stage will bridge the relational mindset to MongoDB. It will cover topics like **why joins are expensive in distributed systems, how document databases change schema design, when normalization helps versus hurts, and why MongoDB encourages different modeling decisions**.

I believe this will make the rest of the roadmap much more intuitive because you'll understand not just *how* MongoDB differs from SQL, but *why* those differences exist from a system design perspective.
